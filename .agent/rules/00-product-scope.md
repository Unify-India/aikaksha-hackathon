# Product Scope Rule

Build only the two hackathon workflows: conversational exam creation/assignment and subjective answer-sheet evaluation/annotation.

Do not expand into attendance, fees, full ERP, adaptive practice, marketplace, live proctoring, or a replacement LMS during the eight-hour build.

Reuse Abhyas only after locating and verifying the actual implementation. A dossier status marked "done" is a discovery lead, not proof that a callable service exists.
